# Rental Warehouse

[日本語](README.md) | **English**

A RimWorld 1.7 mod about storing other factions' goods, defending them from raids and visiting independent vault sites. **Actual in-game behavior, including UI, AI and balance, and compatibility with other mods are unverified.**

## Installation

Requires RimWorld 1.7.4985 and [Harmony](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2009463077). DLC is optional. Extract `RentalWarehouse.zip` from [Releases](https://github.com/pengin0503/rimworld-Rental-warehouse/releases), place its `RentalWarehouse` folder in RimWorld's `Mods` folder, and enable it after Harmony.

It can be added to an existing save, but **mid-save removal is unsupported**: contracts or vault data may no longer load. Back up saves before trying it.

## How to play

Build a reception desk under Misc and start the business. Review and accept offers in the Rental Warehouse tab every 1–3 days. Store delivered crates indoors on shelves or in vaults within one day to begin a 3–12 day contract. The advance becomes available after the term starts; after maturity, return crates by collection pod to receive the remaining storage fee and completion reward. The advance rate can be negotiated once per offer.

Successful returns build trust and unlock larger offers; losses and embezzlement reduce it. Dedicated raiders target stored crates, and crates carried off the map require compensation. Opening a client's crate is embezzlement, requiring compensation equal to its value and reducing goodwill. Unpaid compensation becomes debt and prevents new contracts.

Travel by caravan to independent faction vaults and unlock or destroy them to retrieve goods. With Royalty, eligible colonies can also receive Empire contracts.

## Settings

Mod settings control offer types, vault opportunities, storage-fee and raid-size multipliers and more. Changes apply to new offers and vault information, not ongoing contracts.
